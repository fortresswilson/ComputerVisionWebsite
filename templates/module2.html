05

Perspective Projection Theory

Under the pinhole camera model, the image coordinates of a 3D point depend on its position relative to the camera and the intrinsic camera parameters.

u = fx(X / Z) + cx

v = fy(Y / Z) + cy

Rearranging these equations allows the real-world coordinates to be estimated when depth Z is known.

X = (u - cx)Z / fx

Y = (v - cy)Z / fy

Two-Camera Relationship

If Camera 2 is rotated and translated relative to Camera 1, the coordinates of a 3D point are transformed using:

P₂ = RP₁ + t

R represents the rotation matrix and t represents the translation vector.

s₁x₁ = K₁[I | 0]P

s₂x₂ = K₂[R | t]P

Corresponding image points also satisfy the epipolar constraint:

x₂ᵀ F x₁ = 0
