Module 6: Motion and Structure from Motion

This module explores optical flow, motion tracking, and structure from motion using videos and images captured for the experiment.

Part A: Optical Flow and Motion Tracking

Two videos containing object motion were analyzed. Optical flow was used to estimate the direction and magnitude of motion between consecutive video frames.

Optical Flow

The green motion vectors show how image points move between consecutive frames. Their direction represents the direction of motion, while their length indicates the amount of movement.
